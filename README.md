# HIS 315K Midterm Study Guide

Interactive study guide. Open index.html in a browser. Progress is saved in browser local storage and is not uploaded to GitHub.

Course materials remain the property of their respective authors.

## Narration

Each of the eight sections has a pre-generated MP3 narrated with Luke's existing Chatterbox voice clone. The audio player supports pause, seeking, playback speed, and continuing through the remaining sections. It never starts automatically on page load.

Model weights, studio reference recordings, and credentials are not included. The website plays static audio and does not call a voice API or require a running Windows computer.

## Public study resources (checked October 13, 2026)

The Past exams & sets tab links to a student-uploaded Spring 2011 possible-essay sheet, two Quizlet sets, and three official UT-hosted supplemental lessons. No publicly verifiable Justin Roberts past exam was found. Quizlet full pages were unavailable; only indexed previews were inspected, so their complete answers are not endorsed or reproduced.

Four brief paraphrases of the uploaded essay prompts are integrated into writing practice. Six original short-answer drills and six original quiz questions use the existing course summaries and lecture references. They are labeled as practice rather than recovered exam questions. The existing eight narrated learning sections are unchanged.

## Factual MCQs

An additional 64-question bank provides eight original questions per learning section. It drills people, dates, places, colonies, documents, and events. Topic filters, randomized ten-question sets, full-topic practice, saved answers, retries, and missed-answer review are available. Every answer includes an explanation and an expandable course summary with the original slide references. These are generated practice, not actual exam questions; Tuesday's unposted material remains outside the bank.
